SPARCS Hospital Inpatient Discharges 2024

Source: New York State Department of Health, Statewide Planning and Research Cooperative System (SPARCS)
        https://health.data.ny.gov/d/sf4k-39ay
Size:   2,196,737 rows x 33 columns (one row per inpatient discharge, calendar year 2024)

length_of_stay: Total number of days the patient stayed in the hospital.
                Values 1-119 are exact days but 120 or more days are recorded as 120+ (2,404 rows).
                No missing values. Median = 3, mean = 5.8 when 120+ is treated as 120.

       1-119	Number of days
       120+	120 days or more (top-coded)

                     INJ injury, MBD mental/behavioral, RSP respiratory, GEN genitourinary, XXX unacceptable principal diagnosis (199 rows)
