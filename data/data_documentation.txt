SPARCS Hospital Inpatient Discharges 2024

Source: New York State Department of Health, Statewide Planning and Research Cooperative System (SPARCS)
        https://health.data.ny.gov/d/sf4k-39ay
Size:   2,196,737 rows x 33 columns (one row per inpatient discharge, calendar year 2024)
Types:  Each column below is labeled continuous (unit given), ordinal (categories listed in order),
        or categorical (every category described).

length_of_stay: Total number of days the patient stayed in the hospital.
                Type: continuous. Unit: days.
                Values 1-119 are exact days but 120 or more days are recorded as 120+ (2,404 rows).
                No missing values. Median = 3, mean = 5.8 when 120+ is treated as 120.

       1-119	Number of days
       120+	120 days or more (top-coded)


HOSPITAL / LOCATION

permanent_facility_id: Permanent facility identifier of the hospital (205 values; 5,295 missing)
                       Type: categorical. Each value is a code for one hospital, so there are 205
                       categories. A missing value means the hospital was redacted.

PATIENT

age_group: Age of the patient at admission, in groups
           Type: ordinal. Ordered from youngest to oldest.

       0-17	        0 to 17 years (youngest)
       18-29	       18 to 29 years
       30-49	       30 to 49 years
       50-69	       50 to 69 years
       70 or Older	70 years and older (oldest)

zip_code: First three digits of the patient's ZIP code (50 values; 41,972 missing, 1.91%)
          Type: categorical. Each prefix is a region, and the numbers have no meaningful order.

       100-149	Three digit ZIP prefix of the patient's home area (one category per prefix)
       OOS	       Out of state

gender: Gender of the patient
        Type: categorical.

       F	Female
       M	Male
       U	Unknown (385 rows)

race: Race of the patient
      Type: categorical.

       White	                     White
       Black/African American	Black or African American
       Multi-racial	              More than one race
       Other Race	              Any race not listed above

ethnicity: Ethnicity of the patient
           Type: categorical.

       Spanish/Hispanic	Spanish or Hispanic
       Not Span/Hispanic	Not Spanish or Hispanic
       Multi-ethnic	       More than one ethnicity
       Unknown	       Not known or not reported

ADMISSION AND DISCHARGE

type_of_admission: Type of admission
                   Type: categorical.

       Emergency	Patient needed immediate care for a severe or life threatening condition
       Urgent	       Patient needed prompt care but the condition was not an emergency
       Elective	Planned in advance; the condition allowed time to schedule the stay
       Newborn	Stay of a newborn baby, typically the birth stay
       Trauma	       Admitted as a trauma case, such as a serious injury treated at a trauma center
       Not Available	Admission type was not recorded

emergency_department_indicator: Whether the patient used the emergency department during the stay
                                Type: categorical (two categories).

       Y	Yes, the patient came through the emergency department
       N	No

Clinical

ccsr_diagnosis_code: Clinical Classifications Software Refined (CCSR) category of the principal diagnosis
                     (478 values; no missing values). The prefix gives the body system, for example
                     CIR circulatory, PRG pregnancy, PNL perinatal, DIG digestive, INF infectious,
                     INJ injury, MBD mental/behavioral, RSP respiratory, GEN genitourinary, XXX unacceptable principal diagnosis (199 rows)
                     Type: categorical. Each code is one diagnosis category, so there are 478
                     categories.


apr_medical_surgical: Whether the stay was medical or surgical, according to APR DRG
                      Type: categorical.

       Medical	       Stay grouped as a medical (non-operative) case
       Surgical	       Stay grouped as a surgical case, with an operating room procedure
       Not Applicable	Could not be grouped as medical or surgical (456 rows)

ccsr_procedure_code: CCSR category of the principal procedure (320 values)
                     643,764 missing. Missing only on medical stays (643,617) and
                     not applicable stays (147); every surgical stay has a procedure.
                     Type: categorical. Each code is one procedure category, so there are 320
                     categories. A missing value means no procedure was recorded.

PAYMENT

payment_typology_1: Primary expected source of payment (227 missing, 0.01%)
                    Type: categorical.

       Medicare	              Federal health insurance, mainly for people 65 and older or with disabilities
       Medicaid	              Government health coverage for low income patients
       Private Health Insurance	Commercial insurance plan other than Blue Cross/Blue Shield
       Blue Cross/Blue Shield	Blue Cross/Blue Shield insurance plan
       Self-Pay	              Patient pays directly, with no insurance
       Managed Care, Unspecified	Managed care plan (such as an HMO) with the plan type not specified
       Miscellaneous/Other	       Any other payment source
       Federal/State/Local/VA	Other government programs, including Veterans Affairs
       Department of Corrections	Paid by the Department of Corrections (incarcerated patients)

payment_typology_2: Secondary expected source of payment. Same categories as payment_typology_1
                    1,204,408 missing
                    Type: categorical.

payment_typology_3: Tertiary expected source of payment. Same categories as payment_typology_1
                    1,956,699 missing
                    Type: categorical.
